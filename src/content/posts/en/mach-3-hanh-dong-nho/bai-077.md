# Cooking a Wholesome Meal for Yourself Is Not Luxury — but the Time You Treat Yourself as Someone Being Cared For

*Article 77 / Stream 3: Small Actions*

---

How many times did you stand in the kitchen alone and utter the question: *Just me alone, eating anything is okay, right?*

And then you open the fridge, pick up an instant noodle pack hurriedly, or order a fast food portion delivered to doorsteps. You eat it right at work desks, eating while scrolling phones, chewing hurriedly while thinking about tomorrow's work.

The meal transpires in fifteen minutes, no sounds of appreciation, no presence pauses.

You view that as saving time. You view that as convenience.

Yet looking deeper into that habit, you will realize a quiet truth: We are typically very kind preparing food for others, yet exceedingly stingy with ourselves.

Having guests coming home, you spend hours picking fresh ingredients, carefully seasoning each dish, arranging plates and bowls visually pleasing. You want others to feel your hospitality and care.

Yet remaining alone, you immediately shrink your needs down to minimum levels.

*I am not that important.*

Eating casually while alone is no simple culinary choice. It is a subtle message you continuously send to your own subconsciousness: that you deserve care merely when others are present.

And time arrived to shelve that thought.

Cooking a wholesome meal for yourself is no luxury of time or money. It is a gentle declaration that: you are precious, and you deserve care by your own hands.

---

## When Eating Transforms into Fuel Loading

In modern living rhythms, we turned eating behavior into a technical activity.

We eat because mealtime arrived. We eat for body to hold sufficient calories operating further. We measure food via protein, carbs, or calories — like pouring gas into a machine.

And eating being merely fuel loading, full cultural and emotional beauty of meals vanishes completely.

You feel no longer fried onion fragrance in pans. You see no green color of freshly boiled vegetables. You savor no natural sweetness of hot rice grains.

You chew sensing no taste. You swallow sensing no warmth.

That unfeeling stance toward food reflects the exact unfeeling stance you grant your body.

Treating your body like a machine needing fuel loading, you force it working to exhaustion, then load cheap fast food for it to continue running. Treating it like a harsh slave-owner treating a hired worker.

Yet your body is no machine.

It is the sole house you own across full life duration. It is where storing full memories, full pain, and full joy of yours.

And cooking for that house a wholesome meal is how you listen and soothe fatigue it shouldered for you all day long.

---

## A Wholesome Meal Needs No Delicacies

Many confuse "wholesome meal" with "expensive meal".

Thinking cooking a wholesome meal means buying expensive ingredients, making complex dishes spending hours. And lacking sufficient time or finances, choosing to skip.

Yet kindness resided never in food price tags.

Kindness resides in attentiveness and love you place into preparation processes.

A wholesome meal for you alone can simply be a hot rice bowl, a smooth green boiled vegetable plate, a golden fried egg, and a fragrant soy sauce bowl.

Yet what creates the difference is how you prepare that dish.

That is carefully washing each vegetable leaf under cool water streams.

That is watching flame just right so eggs burn not.

That is wiping clean porcelain plates, carefully scooping rice into bowls, and arranging dishes neatly.

That is cleaning desk corners, folding unfinished laptops, placing phones aside, and sitting down with full presence.

Gazing at the food plate cooked by your own hands, sensing warmth rising up.

Zero rush. Zero casualness. Only you and the meal prepared via full cherishing oneself.

---

## Internal Stance Shift

Sitting down appreciating the wholesome meal cooked by oneself, a deep psychological shift transpires inside you.

You step out of roles perpetually needing to care for external worlds — caring for work, caring for family, caring for social expectations.

You step into positions of someone being cared for.

And the person caring for you is none other than your mature, gentle version.

*Today you labored hard already. Here is the meal I prepared privately for you. Enjoy it and rest.*

That internal dialogue holds healing power larger than any external praise.

It heals the child inside you — the child having felt abandoned, having thought worthy merely when pleasing others.

Self-cooking a wholesome meal, you send a powerful message to subconsciousness: *I need not wait for someone to come love or care for me to feel complete. I hold full capacity granting to oneself that gentleness.*

Emotional self-reliance begins from concrete actions like that.

A person knowing how to cook by oneself a wholesome meal, knowing how to set a clean table, built a peaceful fortress within. Falling no longer into desperate cravings for recognition or love from others, because knowing how to fill oneself their own soul bowl daily.

---

## Moving Meditation in Warm Kitchens

Kitchens, viewed properly, can become the most wonderful meditation space in your house.

In kitchens, full senses awaken naturally.

Water trickling sounds washing vegetables. Cooking oil sizzling sounds contacting hot pans. Onion, garlic, spice fragrances blending in space. Warm flame sensations spreading on face skin.

Attending to each small operation in kitchens, your mind holds no time left wandering to past or future anxieties.

You are fully present with peeling potatoes, with picking vegetable sprouts.

That is a gentle moving meditation form. Helping pull your scattered mind back to body right in present moments.

And meal finishing, sitting eating in relaxed silence continues that meditation process.

Chewing unhurriedly. Sensing nature's sweetness soaking through tongue tips. Sensing food warmth descending to stomach, spreading nourishing energy to each cell.

Each food bite turns into a thank you. Thanking nature for bringing food, and thanking self for dedicating time caring for one's own life.

---

## Relying on Everyday Kindness

Life out there will hold exceedingly harsh days.

Days rejected by others, treated unfairly, or feeling complete failure.

In those dark days, external worlds seeming to turn backs, warm kitchens and cooked by oneself meals turn into sturdiest refuges.

You might control not how others treat you. You might control not work outputs.

Yet you completely control what you cook for yourself tonight.

Returning home, turning on stoves, cooking yourself a hot soup bowl.

Gazing at thick steam rising from soup bowls, taking a deep breath, sensing a gentle truth: However external worlds collapse, you remain here, safe, and holding capacity to self-care.

That kindness no one can strip from you.

It is a small yet stubborn flame, perpetually burning bright in your soul, chasing away daily loneliness and coldness.

Tonight, or tomorrow, dedicate thirty minutes.

Eat casually no longer. Fuel-load hurriedly no longer.

Step into kitchens, cook a simple dish you like, arrange it on the most beautiful plate, and treat yourself like the most honored guest ever hosted in your life.
