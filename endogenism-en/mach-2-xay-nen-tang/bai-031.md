# Building an internal compass in a noisy world

*Article 31 / Stream 2: Building Foundations*

---

External expectations often act as noisy signposts. Developing an internal compass requires consistent self-reflection and grounded values.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Self-Observation:** Witnessing thoughts without immediate judgment.
2. **Alignment:** Making choices that reflect intrinsic principles rather than external pressure.
