# Ten Minutes of Muscle Stretching — How Long Has Your Body Carried This Tension Un-Remembered?

*Article 82 / Stream 3: Small Actions*

---

Pause right in this moment, right amidst the text lines you are reading.

Direct your gentle attention back to the physical body you reside in.

Where are your two shoulders right now?

Are they rising high, tensing up and aiming toward your ears?

Is your jaw clenched tightly, two row teeth biting together unconsciously?

Is your eyebrow gap frowning, and your chest heaving very short, very shallow breaths?

If answer is yes... exhale a gentle sigh and relax shoulders down.

We live in a world overly valuing minds, thoughts and metrics.

We turn our body into a pure labor tool, a car carrying brains from place to place, from meeting to meeting, deadline to deadline.

Forcing it to sit hours before computer screens, forcing it to absorb fast food, drink dense coffee cups forcing it to stay alert.

And across that rushing journey, a serene yet grim truth exists that we accidentally abandoned.

Your body stores your full emotional history.

Each un-released anxiety, each reluctantly swallowed word, each extinguished rage, each suppressed fear... vanished never.

Silently descending into muscle fibers, ligaments, vertebrae and bone joints, turning into silent and heavy constricting knots.

Your body carried those burdens across weeks, months, or even years... without once getting visited or remembered by you.

---

## Cell's Forgotten Language

Psychiatry and somatic therapy researchers proved: Mind and physical body are no separate entities.

Two sides of the same coin, two languages reflecting each other.

When mind experiences stress, nervous systems immediately emit hazard warning signals.

Your body prepares for fight or flight reactions.

Muscle fibers in neck, shoulders, back and hips automatically constrict forming a protective armor shielding vital internal organs.

Yet in modern life, threats are no ferocious tigers in ancient forests.

Threats are a boss's angry message, an un-paid bill, a future fear or a quarrel with loved ones.

Because threats complete never, your body receives zero safety signals to relax.

That constricting muscle armor stays intact from day to day.

And gradually, accustomed to dull neck-shoulder aches, accustomed to lower back constriction, treating it as "normal things" of adulthood.

Yet that is no normal thing.

That is cells' silent help cries, earnest messages from body:

*“I am very tired... Release me from this burden...”*

## Metaphor of Old Wooden Houses and Rusted Hinges

Imagine your body like an ancient wooden house standing firm across many storm seasons.

Through time, un-opening wooden doors regularly, iron hinges begin clinging dust, moss and rust.

Each time pushing that door open, emitting dry, heavy and painful creaks.

Using brute force kicking doors hard, hinges might snap two and doors damage completely.

What doors need is no rushing forced strength, but smooth oil drops applied gently to each joint, accompanied by slow, patient turns.

Ten minutes gentle stretching daily is those exact gentle oil drops for your body house.

Another metaphor is small streams blocked by dry branches, trash and large rocks floating from headwaters.

Pure water stream unable to circulate, turning stagnant and emitting foul odors.

Stretching muscles, you are no person using hammers breaking streams.

Simply a person patiently hand-picking each rotted branch, shifting small rocks aside...

For blood streams, breath streams and living energy (prana) to freely flow again through each nook, each toe, finger tip of yours.

## Ten Minutes Returning — A Polite Apology

Endogenism philosophy invites approaching 10 minutes stretching not as high-exertion workouts, not to burn calories or achieve standard physiques to boast.

Treat 10 minutes stretching as a sacred return ceremony, a polite and deep apology bestowed upon your body.

Prepare a small quiet space in room.

Demanding no expensive tools, needing merely a soft mat or clean floor space.

Turning off all phone notifications, turning on soothing wordless music melodies or letting space stay silent.

Beginning via sitting cross-legged, keeping spine naturally straight yet un-tensed.

Closing eyes and inhaling deep breaths via nose, air descending to bottom belly, then exhaling gently via mouth.

Whisper inside:

*Body, I returned... Thank you for shouldering alongside me full long days...*

## Healing Journey via Each Gentle Movement

Now, begin moving in an exceedingly slow manner, listening to each smallest muscle fiber reaction.

Slowly tilt head leftward, ear aiming toward left shoulder.

Immediately, sensing muscle cords stretching from under ears down to right shoulder tops.

A sensation mildly painful yet comfortable surging.

That is where past week's anxieties lurk.

Rush not to jerk hard. Hold that posture in 30 seconds, and breathe deep.

Each inhale, imagine bringing fresh air streams and love to that exact constricting knot.

Each exhale, whisper: *“Relax... You are safe now...”*

Afterward, switch rightward.

Next, gently roll shoulders in serene circles. Expanding chest forward, pulling shoulders backward.

Sensing two shoulder blades pressing together like two hands embracing heart from behind backs.

Then lower body down to Child's Pose, forehead touching mats softly, arms extending long forward, hips lowering toward heels.

In this pose, full back stretches open.

Surrendering completely before Earth's gravity. Earth supporting full physical body weight.

Needing no longer to strain holding anything.

Relax belly, relax forehead, relax lower back zones.

Each exhale is a time shedding a tension layer, a vague fear down to ground.

## Releasing Concealed Emotional Knots

A miraculous phenomenon routinely occurs practicing deep and quiet muscle stretching.

When muscle fibers constrict across months suddenly relax and stretch open completely...

A sudden emotional wave surges up.

Perhaps eyes sting, an unconscious tear rolls down cheeks.

Perhaps old memories, forgotten sadness or sudden self-pity sensations flood back.

Panic not nor suppress those reactions.

That is when muscle tissues release trapped emotional energy (somatic release).

Overly long suppressed pain found a safe exit stepping out of physical bodies.

Smile and welcome those tears.

That is evidence showing your body trusts you, feeling safe beside you to shed defense armor.

After 15 brief minutes, slowly opening eyes and standing up...

Feeling footsteps growing vastly lighter.

Air entering chest deeper, wider.

Mind turning clear, sharp and strangely quiet.

Mind clutched no longer by negative thinking loops, because physical body just freed from constricting dungeons.

## Turning Ten Minutes into Daily Sanctuaries

Ten minutes muscle stretching costs no excessive time or effort.

Demanding no extraordinary exertion or iron discipline.

Demanding merely gentleness and self-love sufficiently large.

Turn these 10 minutes into an indispensable habit before sleep or after waking.

That is time pausing all social roles: no longer employee, no longer parent, responsible child...

Simply a living organism conversing and caring for one's own physical body house.

Remember: You hold solely this single body to live a complete human life.

That house traveled alongside you through storms, endured wounds and labored each second keeping you living.

Treat it with highest appreciation and deep gratitude.

Ten minutes relaxation today is the most wonderful gift bestowed upon your life's most loyal companion.
