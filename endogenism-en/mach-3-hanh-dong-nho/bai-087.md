# Who You Follow on Social Media Will Shape What You See Daily — and What You See Daily Shapes How You View the World

*Article 87 / Stream 3: Small Actions*

---

Each morning waking up, what is your initial action?

For most of us, that is reaching for phones, opening a social media app and beginning scrolling. In the subsequent 10, 15 or 30 minutes, your eyes absorb dozens of images, status lines, short videos and news.

We think that is merely a harmless entertainment action starting fresh days. Yet truth is: this tiny action silently resets your brain's reality filter.

Social media is no mere communication tool; it is a virtual reality creation machine. And who draws that reality for you is precisely the account list you choose to follow.

What they post, values they promote, arguments they join... all create daily spiritual food for your brain. And that food source shapes how you perceive external worlds and your own worth.

---

## Reality Filters and Social Media Algorithms

Our brain holds an info-filtering mechanism named **Reticular Activating System (RAS)**. This system decides which info among millions of surrounding stimuli is permitted into your awareness.

Focusing continuously on something, RAS automatically scans surroundings seeking similar items.

Scrolling social media, you train your RAS via app algorithms. Algorithms hold a single goal: keeping you inside apps as long as possible. And the best way doing so is displaying content triggering strongest emotions — typically envy, anxiety, outrage, or comparison.

Following:
- Influencers continuously boasting lavish lifestyles, luxurious trips and expensive designer items... your RAS gets programmed to believe **the world is where everyone is wealthy and joyful**, except you. Everywhere you look, you see your own deficiency.
- Sensational news pages, toxic exposure and argument groups... your RAS will believe **the world is a place packed with hazards, deceit and injustice**. Viewing everyone via doubt and defense.
- People continuously talking about success pressure, million-dollar startups at age 20, or working 16 hours daily... your RAS programs fear that **you are falling behind and lazy**, even while striving your best daily.

Your reality is not what occurs out there. Your reality is what you pay attention to. And your following list is that exact attention controller.

---

## Revolution from Unfollow Buttons

We typically think altering worldviews or psychological healing demands grand therapies, expensive retreats or giant changes.

Yet occasionally, the most revolutionary action for your mental health is simply: **tidying your social media following list.**

This is a small, practical action, bearing exceedingly deep impacts. It is actively regaining control over mind input sources.

Begin an attention purification campaign via scrolling your following list and asking questions for each account:

> **"This person's content leaves me feeling how after viewing? Nourishing my peace, curiosity, learning, or triggering envy, anxiety and self-judgment?"**

If the answer is the latter, hesitate not clicking **Unfollow** or **Mute**.

Doing so not out of hating them. Doing so because loving and protecting your soul's internal silence. Holding zero obligation consuming content harming your peace.

---

## Redesigning Mind Food Sources

After tidying toxic energy sources, actively seek and follow accounts bringing real value to your "returning to self" journey:

- Follow people sharing deep knowledge, slow philosophical angles, helping expand thinking rather than merely triggering short-term emotions.
- Follow artists, nature lovers, sharing simple, serene moments of routine life.
- Follow mindfulness, self-compassion communities, where reminded of your unconditional self-worth.

Altering following lists, your spiritual food source alters. Your RAS gets re-programmed.

Gradually, you realize seeing beautiful items, slow moments and more tolerant perspectives in routine real life. Surrounding worlds abruptly turn gentler and vastly more comfortable.

---

## Practice for Today

Right now, open the social media app you use most:

1. Enter "Following" lists.
2. Select at least **5 accounts** routinely leaving you restless, anxious, or self-comparing to click **Unfollow** or **Mute**.
3. Search and follow **2 additional accounts** sharing about slow living, nature or peaceful living philosophy.

Your attention is your most precious asset. Grant it not for free to toxic algorithms. Protect it, cherish it, and direct it toward what truly nourishes your soul.
