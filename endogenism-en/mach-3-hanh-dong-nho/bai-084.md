# Bored Chores Can Be Empty Gaps — and Empty Gaps Are Where Vital Items Spring

*Article 84 / Stream 3: Small Actions*

---

You stand before sink basins after a solitary dinner.

Stacked dishes and cups await your hands to clean.

Or standing folding each dried shirt and pants in laundry baskets.

Or waiting in line at supermarkets, or walking on familiar paths traveled thousands of times.

Right in those moments, a familiar feeling immediately surges.

Boredom. Sluggishness. Impatience.

Your mind begins complaining:

*“Why are these tasks so boring and time-wasting... Must finish fast to do other tasks... Or turn on phones, play a video or podcast to listen while working to lessen boredom...”*

And via modern humans' automatic reflexes, your hands immediately reach for phones.

Scrolling short news feeds, watching 15-second colorful videos, loading continuous stimulating info streams into brains.

Unable to endure 5 empty minutes. Unable to stay still in repeating task silence.

We live in a world panicked before boredom.

Mistaking empty gaps as waste, deficits needing immediate filling via sounds, images and data.

Yet Endogenism philosophy offers a completely reversed perspective.

Bored tasks are no burdens or punishments.

They are rare and invaluable empty gaps life intentionally bestows — and right in that quiet empty gap, truly vital items hold opportunities to sprout.

---

## Fear of Empty Gaps in Overloaded Worlds

Why fear empty minutes so intensely?

Because lacking any external stimuli — zero phones, zero music, zero talking people...

Brains automatically redirect attention inward.

And for many, looking inward is a scary experience.

There, in empty gaps' silence, suppressed anxieties, un-answered existential questions, old loneliness or hurt sensations sprout to surface.

Running away from those insecure whispers, we choose addiction to busyness.

Using rushing info streams from social media to numb senses, turning minds into waste-stowing warehouses of the world.

Yet what is the price paid for continuous gap filling?

Mental exhaustion. Vanishing of deep creative capacity.

Brains stuffed continuously with info from waking until closing eyes for sleep... holding zero square centimeters of empty gap left to digest, connect and generate fresh ideas.

Your mind resembles a room packed with items from floor to ceiling. Unable to even open doors stepping in, let alone finding hidden precious gems.

---

## Metaphor of Muddy Lakes and Seeds in Dark Soils

To understand empty gaps' transformative power, let's look at two natural metaphors together.

Imagine a lake surface continuously stirred via a large pole.

Bottom mud rolled up, turning full water streams into a muddy brown color.

Continuing stirring, dropping fresh rocks into lakes (equivalent to loading fresh info)... water streams stay muddy forever.

Unable to see lake bottoms, unable to see coral reefs or small swimming fish schools.

Sole way rendering lake water clear again is no striving to filter each mud particle.

Sole way is pulling poles out and letting lake surfaces stay completely quiet.

Lacking any stirring, via natural laws, each mud particle automatically settles to bottoms.

Water streams turn transparent like mirrors, reflecting white sky clouds above.

Bored tasks like washing dishes, sweeping houses, walking... are when pulling poles out of mind lake surfaces.

Another metaphor is seed sprouting processes.

Small seeds sprout never in noisy places, shimmering under disco lamps or flat concrete roads.

Needing burial into dark moist soil layers, in ground silence, discretion and emptiness.

Silences and darkness of underground empty gaps are sole environments supplying sufficient nutrients for seed coats to shatter, for green sprouts to reach welcoming suns.

Creative ideas, solutions to life deadlocks, and authentic peace... are those exact green sprouts.

Sprouting merely in quiet empty gaps of your soul.

---

## Default Mode Networks — Where Wisdom Idles Self

Modern neuroscience discovered an exceedingly interesting brain mechanism named Default Mode Network (DMN).

Concentrating intensely on complex tasks like math solving, report writing or programming... DMN turns off temporarily.

Yet doing simple, repeating manual tasks — tasks demanding zero complex logic thinking — DMN lights up automatically.

This is the exact state where brains execute transcendent subtle connections.

Self-searching in memory archives, connecting un-related data, processing rushing emotions and self-locating solutions to hard problems deadlocked pastly.

Why Archimedes invented famous buoyancy not while sitting at tense work desks, but floating still in bathtubs.

Why Newton realized universal gravitation laws sitting staring at falling apples under tree bases.

Why most wonderful creative ideas, life-altering decisions abruptly spring standing under showers, washing dishes, or walking aimlessly on roads.

Bored tasks steal zero time of yours.

Liberating your mind from Ego's desperate yoke (the Controlling Self), letting subterranean wisdom freely execute its grand creation work.

---

## Transforming Bored Tasks into Meditation Sanctuaries

How to transform exhausting repeating tasks into soul-nourishing empty gaps?

Secret lies in full presence and welcoming attitudes.

Washing a porcelain cup...

Stow phones into another room.

Rush not finishing to do other tasks. Turn cup washing into life's sole purpose in those 2 minutes.

Feel warm water streams flowing past finger gaps.

Listen to soap bubbles crackling gently on ceramic surfaces.

Gaze at stains fading and cup smooth shine surfacing under kitchen lamps.

Touching tasks via whole-hearted attention, that task is "bored" no longer.

Transforming into a deep meditation ritual (mindfulness in action).

Daydreaming no longer over futures, tearing no longer pasts. Present here, complete with water streams and cups.

And right in that presence gap, a gentle peace wave will spread from finger tips through your full body.

---

## Welcoming Whispers from Empty Gaps

Actively preserving empty gaps daily without rushing to fill them...

Prepare spirits to listen to sacred whispers from eye bottoms.

Perhaps folding a shirt, forgiveness unexpectedly surges in heart toward someone having hurt you.

Perhaps waiting at red traffic lights, suddenly realizing living overly rushed and needing slowness.

Perhaps walking under tree lines, a work crisis solution abruptly surfaces clearly, simple and surprisingly refined.

Those realizations are un-purchasable via money, un-findable on Google or social media.

Priceless gifts of intrinsic wisdom, granted solely to those holding sufficient courage preserving quiet empty gaps for their soul.

Chase away boredom no longer. Hate repeating routine tasks no longer.

Smile welcoming them like loyal soulmates bringing you space to breathe.

Earth needs gap intervals between seasons for trees to flower.

Music needs gap intervals between notes to create graceful melodies.

And your soul needs bored empty gaps for truly vital and sacred items permitted to sprout.
