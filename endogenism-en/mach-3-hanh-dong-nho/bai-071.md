# Breathing — the Sole Thing You Did Right from the Initial Second and Never Remembered

*Article 71 / Stream 3: Small Actions*

---

The initial moment you uttered a cry welcoming life, your tiny chest heaved receiving the initial air stream. That was the opening action for your human journey on this earth.

And many years later, when this journey approaches its conclusion, your final executed action will also be a gentle exhale.

Between those two time milestones — between initial cries and final sighs — you inhale and exhale hundreds of millions of times.

You breathe while sleeping soundly, breathe while feeling joyful, breathe while sobbing in pain, and breathe even while submerged deep in most deadlocked anxieties.

Yet how many times do you pause to thank your own breathing rhythm?

We are overly busy chasing grand items, busy chastising oneself over mistakes, to the point of forgetting an obvious truth: breathing is the sole thing you did perfectly right from the initial moment, and never stopped even for a single moment.

This article teaches no complex meditation technique nor medical-standard breathing method.

It serves merely as a quiet pause moment, for you to personally touch your chest, recognizing breathing's silent loyalty — the item silently holding life for you daily.

---

## Self-Judgment and What Never Failed

We humans hold a very harsh habit: spending most memory capacity recording our own failures.

You remember very clearly a time ruining a work plan. You chastise yourself over a slipped word with loved ones. You chastise yourself for lacking sufficient money, lacking an ideal physique, or without becoming the perfect version society expects.

Daily, you grade oneself via countless harsh rulers and typically grant yourself negative scores.

*Certain times, I feel like everything I do is wrong, every path I choose is deadlocked...*

Yet right in the moment feeling like the most tragic failure, your chest still heaves in a rhythmic manner.

Breathing never judges you. It cares not what achievement you just touched or what large error you committed. It demands no degree, no money in accounts, nor maintaining a positive attitude before continuing to flow into your lungs.

It is present unconditionally.

Even if you forget it, treat your body poorly, or sink into despair, breathing persistently supplies oxygen nourishing your each cell. It is the most loyal and forgiving witness for your existence on earth.

---

## Life's Inevitable Forgetting

Why do we so easily forget breathing?

Because it is so natural, so quiet, and never demands attention.

We remember breathing merely when it encounters mishaps: when out of breath after a long run, when throat gets blocked from a stuffy nose, or when a panic attack strikes leaving chest constricting.

Only when threat of losing breath exists do we exclaim realizing how precious breathing is.

This resembles how we treat many other precious items in life. We treat health presence, roof presence, loved ones presence as default, until they risk leaving us.

We live in a noise world. Phone screens roar, work demands, society urges. All those items continuously pull your attention outward.

And thus, breathing — the sole connecting thread holding you with the present — gets pushed into oblivion shadows.

You breathe in a shallow and hurried manner, like a pure biological reflex rather than a soul's vivid experience.

---

## A Conscious Breath in Quietness

Right now, while reading these lines, permit yourself a small pause.

Need not alter seating posture, need not close eyes or seek an absolute quiet space.

Merely shift your attention from the screen back to your chest.

Inhale a breath very slowly... feel cool air streams passing through nose, flooding down throat and expanding your chest.

And then slowly exhale... feel gentle relaxation as warm air exits, shoulders automatically drop, face muscles slowly stretch out.

*Merely one breath, yet seems the full world just paused a few seconds...*

The moment recognizing your breath, you stepped out of wandering thought streams. You are no longer in the past with regrets, nor in the future with anxieties.

You are right here. In this moment. Where your body lives and breath nourishes you.

A conscious breath resolves not immediately your financial or work troubles, but brings you an instant safe space to breathe and recover.

---

## Returning to the Most Primal Home

Many among us spend a lifetime seeking a belonging sensation, a place to call home.

We seek it in an expensive house, in a romantic relationship, or in a solemn social status.

Yet all those external homes can be shaken via life mishaps.

Breathing is the most primal and safest home you perpetually carry with yourself everywhere, anytime.

Whether in a cramped rental room, on a crowded bus, or amidst an intense pressure-filled meeting, merely returning to breathing, you immediately step through doors returning to your internal home.

That place holds no boss judgment, no bill pressure, no world expectations.

That place holds merely unfolding life's rhythmic, serene nature.

Knowing how to rely on breathing, you will no longer feel overly stranded amidst external world waves. You realize perpetually holding a sturdy anchor right inside your chest.

---

## Breathing Rhythm as a Release Reminder

Observing breathing mechanisms closely, you learn the deepest lesson on releasing.

You cannot merely inhale forever. However fresh and wonderful that air stream be, striving to hold it in lungs overly long, it turns toxic choking you.

You are forced to exhale. You must release old air streams to hold space receiving fresh air streams.

Our life also operates via that exact principle.

Past emotions, old experiences, yesterday's resentments or achievements... all resemble processed air streams in lungs. Striving to clutch them tightly, your soul grows suffocating and exhausted.

Each exhale is a time practicing small releases.

Exhaling shoulders' compressed tension. Exhaling internal resentments. Exhaling forced control over items not belonging to your authority.

And then afterward, a fresh inhale slowly floods in, bringing freshness, new energy, and a fresh opportunity to start fresh.

---

## Thanking Silent Loyalty

Tonight, before dropping into sleep, spend a few seconds executing a silent gratitude ritual.

Place your right palm on your left chest.

Feel heart's serene beat and chest's gentle heaving following each breathing rhythm.

Whisper to your body a thank you: *Thank you for perpetually breathing for me. Thank you for not quitting, even on days I most wished to quit.*

You might have failed in many exams, might have hurt someone, might have unachieved grand dreams.

Yet your still sitting here, chest still heaving, and blood still flowing, is the strongest evidence showing you did right the most vital thing: maintaining life.

And from that solid foundation — from a complete and peaceful breath — you hold full strength to step forward on the path ahead.
