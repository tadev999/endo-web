# A Thank You Does Not Change Reality — but It Changes Your Position in That Reality

*Article 66 / Stream 3: Small Actions*

---

In recent years, "gratitude" has become a fashionable phrase mentioned everywhere in self-development books and psychology courses.

People teach you to write according to "ten daily gratitude things" lists. People advise you to smile thanking even when experiencing a painful event, a fraud scam, or a broken breakup.

And that somewhat forced promotion generated a form of "toxic positivity".

It forces you to deny your authentic emotions. Feeling pain or anger, someone telling you "be grateful" feels like nothing less than an insult to the pain you carry.

Your mind rises with completely natural resistance:

*Why must I thank such a terrible event? Thanking won't fix a broken car, will it? Thanking won't return lost money, will it?*

That resistance makes full sense. A thank you holds zero magic rendering real difficulties vanishing.

This sixty-sixth article appears to return authentic, deep nature to thank yous: A thank you alters not the nature of reality out there — but it permanently alters your position in that reality.

---

## Truth Remains Truth

Endogenous philosophy perpetually respects original reality. We never self-deceive via gentle illusions.

A sudden downpour soaking your clothes on the way to work, reality is your clothes are wet and you risk being late. Zero thank you can render your clothes dry immediately.

Betrayed by a close colleague, reality is your trust got hurt and that relationship fractured. Zero thank you can transform that person's wrong behavior into a noble thing.

Facing a loved one's passing, reality is that loss is permanent and the void in your heart is real.

If a thank you gets uttered striving to deny or artificially soothe those truths, it is merely a fragile paint coat covering an un-washed wound.

Yet the problem lies in: Reality is one matter, while **your position facing that reality** is a completely different matter.

Falling into a crisis, you hold two fundamental positions to choose in consciousness.

Position one: A passive victim's stance.

Position two: An active, compassionate observer's stance.

And a thank you is precisely the bridge moving your transition from position one to position two.

---

## Victim Stance and Internal Deprivation

Look at what occurs inside the mind choosing to stand at a victim's position.

Standing at a victim's position, your awareness lens shrinks completely to deprivation. You see merely what you lost, what others did wrong to you, and life's unfairness.

Your mind continuously spins with resentful questions:

*Why me? Why does bad luck always strike my head? Why does this world treat me so terribly?*

Viewing reality from that angle, initial pain multiplies ten-fold via helplessness and bitterness sensations.

You view yourself small, fragile, and battered by external forces outside control. You hand your full internal power to the crisis. You turn yourself into a dry harmless leaf whipped everywhere by storms.

And most dangerous is: Victim stance drains your full action energy.

Believing you are a circumstance victim, you hold zero faith or strength seeking to rise and repair the situation.

You get permanently trapped in resentment quagmires.

---

## Metaphor of the Window and Illuminating Angle

To visualize this position shift, imagine standing in a room holding a large window looking out to natural vistas outside.

Outside the window frame is a garden — in that garden fragrant rose flowers bloom brightly, yet sharp thorn bushes and rain mud puddles also exist.

That garden is your life's reality.

Standing in a narrow dark room corner, un-reached by light, you see merely dark silhouettes of thorn bushes and mud puddles reflecting through glass panes. You conclude this garden is completely terrible and dark.

Yet taking a few steps, approaching the wide open window frame and receiving sunshine — does the external garden vista alter?

No. Thorn bushes remain there. Mud puddles remain there.

Yet the new position permits you to see also rose flowers glowing under sunshine. You see serene white cloud streaks in the sky. You see life continuing miraculously beside mud puddles.

A thank you holds zero duty clearing away thorn bushes or flattening mud puddles in life's garden.

A thank you is the step bringing you near the bright window — where you restore full panoramic, balanced views of reality.

---

## Micro Thank You's True Nature

So, what does an authentic thank you look like under endogenous views?

It is no tragic thank you. It is a thank you directed toward **what remains** and **silent supports** present right beside tragedy.

Experiencing a failed, exhausting workday, you thank not that failure.

You thank feet remaining resilient bearing your body's weight throughout eight hours. You thank the warm water cup warming your palms. You thank still possessing a safe roof to return to lay back.

Hurt by someone, you thank not their terrible behavior.

You thank the deep lesson helping you see human nature clearer. You thank boundary awareness capacity you trained. And you thank other sincere friends standing beside supporting you.

*This event is truly terrible and painful. Yet right beside it, I still receive support from these simple things...*

That thank you holds zero force or lies.

It is clear-minded acknowledgment that: Life was never completely terrible. Even in darkest days, tiny light streaks silently exist — if you courageously turn to acknowledge them.

---

## Consciousness Position Shift

The moment uttering a sincere micro thank you, your position in reality alters immediately.

You are no longer a deprived victim. You become a **subject capable of recognizing and appreciating**.

From feeling poor and unfortunate, you realize still owning countless priceless spiritual assets: breath, awareness, compassion, and living attitude choice capacity.

This shift returns to you full internal power.

You waste energy no longer on anger, resentment over past events. You concentrate that energy on caring for the present and constructing the future.

Stoic philosopher Marcus Aurelius reminded himself in his personal journal: "Be like the cliff face against which waves crash continuously. It stands firm, and raging waves around it gradually settle."

A thank you is precisely the glue compacting your internal cliff face.

It holds you from getting swept away by circumstance volatility waves. It bestows upon you a sturdy position from which to view storms with a calm, tolerant smile.

---

## End-of-Day Thank You Practice

Turn thank yous into a natural small action each evening before sleep.

Force not yourself seeking grand things. Seek things small to an almost invisible degree during the day.

Thank the bowl holding your dinner.

Thank the bus driver bringing you home safely.

Thank a cool breeze sweeping forehead walking on sidewalks.

Thank yourself for not quitting during an un-easy day.

Speak those thank yous in your soul's silence. Feel lightened sensations spreading in your chest releasing reality resistance.

External reality may still hold many difficulties awaiting you tomorrow. Life never promised rose paths for anyone.

Yet you will step into tomorrow with a completely different position: A peaceful, sturdy, compassion-flooded human position — a human comprehending always holding rights choosing how to view one's own life.
