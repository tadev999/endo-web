# Water Flows Past You Under the Shower — and with It, Perhaps Something Else Is Also Permitted to Float Away

*Article 89 / Stream 3: Small Actions*

---

After a long day pensively facing external worlds, you return home, close doors and step into bathrooms.

Water splashes as you turn shower valves. Warm or cool water streams begin flowing, touching head tops, rolling past tired shoulders, descending down spines and flowing past your skin.

Routinely, we view showering merely as pure personal hygiene actions — daily repeating living habits where minds are rarely truly present.

While water flows on physical bodies, our mind might still race with unfinished tasks, afternoon arguments, or tomorrow's anxieties.

Yet pausing a beat and permitting self to truly sense this moment...

You will realize showers wash away no mere skin dust.

That water stream can turn into the most silent and deep liberation ritual you privately dedicate to yourself daily.

A place where you wash clean no mere physical body, but can permit mind burdens to float away following water streams down drainpipes.

---

## Physical Bodies as Psychological Scar Warehouses

We typically think negative emotions, stress or sadness reside merely in thoughts.

Yet actually, your body is the initial house shouldering full those pressures.

Feeling angry or anxious, shoulders automatically tense up. Feeling resentful or fearful, chest tightens, breathing turning shallow and rushed.

Across long 8 to 12 hour days, your body resembles a sponge absorbing full tense atmospheres of offices, streets, complex relationships.

*Seems my shoulders are breaking tired... Why is my chest so heavy?*

Physical traces left by emotions on your body.

Stepping out of a work day carrying intact stress onto beds, feeling restless, un-comfortable and unable to rest truly.

Needing a transition space. A place to shed the heavy armor worn full long days.

And the most wonderful, simplest transition space is water streams under showers.

---

## Washing Rituals Under Water Streams

Water is one of this planet's oldest and most natural healing elements.

Water holds capacity to embrace your full body surface simultaneously. Water holds softness, rhythm, and un-tiring washing strength.

Standing under showers, close eyes and direct full attention back to touch sensations.

Sense each water drop's weight as contacting your skin. Sense warmth dissolving on constricting shoulders.

And along with water flowing down, practice a silent visualization act.

Visualize today's fatigue, hurtful words poured onto you, self-doubt or anxiety...

All crystallizing into a thin dust layer on your skin.

*Let this water stream wash it away... I need not keep this thing longer.*

Water flowing from head down feet into drainpipes, watch it.

Whisper softly to self: that fatigue finished. Belonging to today, and today concluded under this water stream. Permitted no further following you into silent bedrooms.

---

## Permitting Yourself to Shed Armor

In external worlds, you play many roles.

Professional employee, decisive leader, obedient child, or listening friend.

Keeping face looking calm, appearing okay, controlling emotions to avoid bothering others.

Yet in bathrooms, under showers, that separating wall collapses completely.

Sole place no one views you, no one evaluates you, and no one demands anything from you.

Permitted shedding completely the strong armor shouldered full days.

*Wishing to cry, let tears blend into shower streams... No one knows, and completely okay.*

*Wishing to exhale a long deep sigh, exhale as much as possible.*

Water embraces your sighing sounds. Water washes away warm tears. Water asks no reasons, nor demands your explanations.

Water's silent tolerance permits returning to be a most primal human — fragile, tired yet completely honest with self.

---

## Transforming Routine Habits into Serene Meditations

Endogenism demands no spending hours sitting meditating on expensive cushions or traveling to far silent places.

Internal life's true cultivation resides in bringing conscious presence into routine's smallest actions.

Showering under showers is a wonderful opportunity for that.

Rather than viewing 15 bathroom minutes as dead time to catch up thinking about work...

Turn those 15 minutes into a tactile meditation period.

Smell soap bar or body wash fragrances. Sense soft snow bubbles on skin.

Listen to water trickling sounds like rain falling in old-growth forests.

*Right now, only me and water streams. Zero work, zero future, zero past.*

Fully present with water streams, your brain receives an exceedingly clear safety signal.

Nervous systems begin cooling down. Muscle fibers begin relaxing. Silent stress accumulated since morning gradually dissolves.

---

## Stepping Out via a Fresh Essence

Turning off shower valves and water streams stopping, spend a few seconds standing still in bathroom silence.

Taking dry towels gently wiping each body part.

Drying body is also a silent assertion: caring for your physical temple.

Looking into steam-veiled mirrors, seeing your image reflected through.

Holding perhaps remaining life fatigue, yet different from the person having stepped into bathrooms 15 minutes prior.

Washed clean. Shelved a part of burdens.

*Returned to today what belongs to today. Right now, clean and lightened.*

Stepping out of bathrooms in comfortable clothes, feeling bedrooms seemingly gentler, beds seemingly plushier.

And most importantly, your soul ready for a complete sleep — sleep of a person knowing to pause and permitting old items to float away.

## Before You Read the Next Article

Tonight, stepping into bathrooms and turning shower valves, try removing earphones, turning off music devices.

Stand under water streams, close eyes and whisper a silent word to self: *"This water stream is washing me clean. Full today's fatigue, follow water floating away."*

Observe chest and shoulder sensations as permitting something to truly float away alongside water streams.
