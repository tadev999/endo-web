# You Existed Today — Not Merely Existed, but Existed on a Day Where Not Every Day Is Easy to Do So

*Article 90 / Stream 3: Small Actions*

---

Night has turned late, surrounding space sinking into a vast silence.

You sit alone on bed edges, or leaning back on familiar chairs after a long pensive and exhausting day.

Closing eyes and looking back at past 24 hours just elapsed, a silent question routine surfaces in mind:

*What did I accomplish today? Did I finish grand goals, reach any significant achievements?*

And if the answer is "no" — if today you created zero work breakthroughs, finished no to-do list, even spent most time fighting sadness or overload...

A subtle feeling of guilt and disappointment easily invades you. Feeling you wasted a day, feeling inadequate and standing still.

Yet this article — the final article of Stream 3: Small Actions — wishes to tell you something completely different.

If the sole thing accomplished today was simply: continuing to breathe, continuing to be present, and not letting go before subterranean storms...

Then that was a grand victory already.

You existed today — not merely existed superficially, but courageously existed on a day where keeping oneself intact was no easy matter.

---

## The "Daily Productivity" Standard Trap

We live in a culture obsessed with productivity and continuous achievements.

Each day stepping into streets or opening social media, bombarded with messages on breaking through, optimizing time, becoming better than yesterday.

Society measures a day's value via numbers: tasks finished, money earned, project count approved.

And implicitly assuming days you create no measurable value are "meaningless days".

That harsh thinking leaves us forgetting a fundamental truth of human existence:

Humans are no biological machines programmed to operate at maximum capacity 365 days a year.

We are living entities holding rhythms. We hold energy-filled springs, yet also hold exhausted and freezing winters.

*Certain days, merely waking, stepping out of bed and smiling at the world demanded full strength owned...*

Having experienced such a day, be tolerant of yourself.

Use no brilliant sunny day metric to evaluate a day forced to walk through storms.

Surviving through a terrible day is no surrender. That is soul's most silent resilient form.

---

## Courage of Not Giving Up

We typically link "courage" with dragon-slaying actions, grand declarations before crowds, or fate-altering decisions.

Yet another courage type exists, vastly more silent, un-applauded by anyone and un-seen by anyone.

That is courage when feeling heart heavy, chest constricting via anxiety, yet choosing to take a deep breath and step forward.

That is courage when wishing to let go of everything, yet gently self-saying: *"Strive a bit more, past tonight everything will differ."*

That is courage of not giving up on yourself.

*Does anyone know how I struggled passing through past 24 hours? No one. Yet I know.*

And that self-knowing suffices. Needing no medals or external praise to prove you were courageous.

Your remaining here tonight, breathing rhythm steady, heart beating earnest beats...

Vivid proof showing your internal strength is vastly larger than hurdles just experienced.

## Looking Back at Stream 3 Journey: Gentle Movements

Thirty articles of Stream 3 (Article 61 to Article 90) accompanied you through a special journey: journey of Small Actions.

We learned together brewing a warm water glass and drinking in complete presence.

We learned taking five deep breaths when anger or overload struck. Learned tidying a small desk corner to regain mind order.

Learned writing down cluttered thoughts, stretching tired muscles, turning off phones before sleep, smiling at strangers, and showering under showers as washing rituals.

And reaching this article, we touch the smallest yet grandest action: PERMITTING ONESELF TO EXIST.

*Full those small actions... will they truly alter my life?*

Yes. Altering your life via no large explosion or overnight miracle.

Altering your life via constructing a sturdy internal structure from within.

Teaching you: peace resides in no far-off vista when full difficulties end.

Peace resides in your capacity self-caring, self-relying right amidst reality's fluctuations.
