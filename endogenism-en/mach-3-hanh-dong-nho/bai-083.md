# Start Writing Even When Not Knowing What You Are Writing — Because Sometimes You Need to Start to Know What You Are Thinking

*Article 83 / Stream 3: Small Actions*

---

You sit before a pure white paper page, or before a computer screen blinking a soul-less cursor.

The pen rests still on desks, and your hand hovers in mid-air.

Inside your mind is a bizarre chaos.

You feel your head flooded with turmoil, vague anxieties and endless thinking streams entwining like a tangled thread.

Yet striving to grab a thread to begin writing something down... your mind unexpectedly turns blank.

Self-critical voices immediately utter:

*“I don't know what I'm thinking to write? Write what now? My words are so naive, my ideas so cluttered... Wait until thinking completes clearly then write...”*

And thus, you fold notebooks, placing pens down.

You continue carrying that tangled thread through a full day, letting it silently torment and drain your energy.

We routinely fall into a classic thinking illusion: Believing we must hold complete, coherent and clear thoughts *before* we begin writing down.

Yet Endogenism philosophy and mind liberation arts assert a completely opposite truth.

Writing is no recording act of what completed in head.

Writing is precisely the ongoing thinking process. Writing is the sole path bringing thoughts concealed deep in darkness out to light.

Sometimes, forced to begin placing pens writing even when un-knowing what writing — because only when pens move, does your mind begin discovering what it truly carries.

---

## Vagueness and the Tangled Thread in Head

Why does merely sitting thinking idly in head rarely bring clarity?

Because human brains were designed un-for storing and arranging thousands of complex thinking streams simultaneously.

Striving to resolve an issue or sadness via tossing and turning thoughts in head...

Thinking streams tend to run endless loops (ruminating loops).

A negative thought appears, dragging past fears, twisting to future anxieties, then returning to tear present moments.

Overlapping each other, creating a thick fog cloud leaving you feeling suffocated and deadlocked.

Thinking in head resembles striving to arrange 100 furniture items in a pitch-black room.

Bumping everywhere encountering tangles, breakages and pain.

White paper pages are turning on bright lamps and bringing each item out to spacious front yards.

When a thought gets written into text lines via ink or surfacing on screens, it officially exits your internal structure.

Ceasing to be part of you. Transforming into an external object you can calmly observe, analyze and name.

And the moment initial threads get pulled from tangled threads, full deadlocks begin self-unraveling.

---

## Metaphor of Rusted Water Valves and Night Forest Paths

To imagine the magic of "writing un-needing to know", let's imagine two vivid metaphors together.

Initial image is a mechanical water valve abandoned long in an old house.

Turning that valve open initial times after many years...

Expect no pure, cool mineral water stream immediately gushing out.

In initial few dozen seconds, what flows from that valve is muddy brown water, mixed with rust, sediment and annoyed gurgling sounds.

An impatient person seeing that dirty water, panicked closing valves immediately saying: *“Oh, this valve broke, water full of trash!”*

Yet a knowledgeable person patiently lets valves continue flowing.

Knowing that muddy water layer must flow clean before clearing pipes.

And after 1-2 patient minutes, pure, clear groundwater streams from deep earths begin flooding out, cool and sweet.

Your paper pages are completely similar.

Initial text lines written might be very naive, nonsense, absurd and full of self-blame.

That is merely rust water layers discharging from mind pipes.

Courageously discharge full rust water layers, for intrinsic wisdom streams beneath to hold opportunities to reveal.

Another metaphor is journeys passing through old forests at night holding storm lamps in hand.

That tiny storm lamp cannot illuminate full 10-kilometer paths before you.

Illuminating merely exact single footsteps right before your eyes.

Standing still at forest entrances demanding to see full paths before stepping... staying trapped forever in darkness.

Yet courageously stepping initial steps into that small light patch... lamps automatically illuminate subsequent steps.

Writing is that exact storm lamp. Needing no ending views, needing merely initial word writing.

---

## Turning Off Harsh Internal Editor Voices

Largest hurdle preventing us from starting writing is "The Internal Editor" presence.

A harsh, perfectionist voice perpetually lurking inside your head.

Just writing down a word, Internal Editors immediately judge:

*“Writing so stupid! Spelling error there! Using this word looks cheap! This article holds zero value!”*

Evaluation fear — even evaluated by oneself — leaves you completely paralyzed.

Endogenism philosophy invites executing a liberation act: Courageously chase Internal Editors out of work rooms in 15 minutes.

Self-promise a sacred commitment:

*What I write in these 15 minutes is completely private. No one on earth permitted to read it, including future versions of me if un-wished. Permitted to write anything, even roughest, most chaotic or nonsense words.*

Permitting yourself to "write poorly", absolute freedom explodes.

Beginning to apply Stream of Consciousness writing methods.

Placing pens down and permitting pens zero pauses even for a single second.

Un-knowing what to write, write that exact sentence onto paper:

*“I am sitting here and completely un-knowing what I am writing. My hand moves but my head is blank. I feel so funny having to write these lines...”*

Continue writing that sentence 5 times, 10 times...

And then, magic occurs.

By 11th time, brains automatically grow bored of repetition, and a subterranean door in subconsciousness automatically opens.

---

## Unexpected Truth Behind Trivial Text Lines

What appears behind that subterranean door leaves you astonished.

Suddenly, beneath trivial word lines, a completely fresh text line surfaces:

*\*...Actually, what bothered me since morning was no colleague's remark, but feeling I am being abandoned...\**

Or:

*\*...Seems long since I permitted myself to cry...\**

Those truths inherently rested ready in subconsciousness long ago.

Yet life being overly noisy, mind overly busy shouldering and concealing, you were unable to hear that whisper.

Moving pens played roles as diligent guides, leading you through surface thinking fog layers to touch intrinsic truth gold ore layers.

Suddenly understanding why hot-tempered, why fatigued, why insecure.

And the second seeing that truth surfacing clearly on paper pages... full previous vagueness vanishes.

Locating answers needing zero external inquiries.

---

## Pens as Late-Night Soulmates

Transform free writing into a sacred communication ritual with your own self.

Whenever heart feels heavy, whenever feeling lost amidst decisions...

Rush not turning on phones seeking distraction, nor rush onto social media venting rage.

Open a small notebook, pick up familiar pens.

Treat paper pages as absolute loyal soulmates, ready to sit listening to you vent at 3 AM un-judging, un-interrupting, un-telling your story to others.

Paper pages contain everything. Embracing tears, rage, naive dreams and your most secret fears.

Writing is how you personally heal internal wounds via your own presence.

Matter not whether sentences flow smoothly, matter not whether handwriting is beautiful.

Sole thing mattering is pens moving, and your soul breathing.

## Before You Read the Next Article

Now, take a small paper sheet or open note apps on phones...

Place fingers down writing continuously in 2 minutes un-thinking, beginning via sentence: *"Right now, what conceals beneath my mind is..."*

Pause not to correct spelling errors, let pens flow freely.

Which truth just silently stepped into light from under your fingers?
