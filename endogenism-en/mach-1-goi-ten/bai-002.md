# The difference between inner silence and emotional numbness

*Article 2 / Stream 1: Naming*

---

Silence can be a refuge, or it can be a warning sign. 

When we step back from external noise, we seek tranquility. But sometimes, what feels like peace is actually an emotional freeze response — a coping mechanism when life feels overwhelming.

---

## Observing the Mind

Learning to distinguish between peaceful stillness and self-protective numbness allows us to remain open to genuine joy and connection.
