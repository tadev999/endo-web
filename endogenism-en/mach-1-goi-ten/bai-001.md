# A type of tiredness not caused by lack of sleep — do you know its name?

*Article 1 / Stream 1: Naming*

---

You get enough sleep. Sometimes even more than usual.

Yet when you wake up, the exhaustion is still there — even heavier than the night before. It is not in your eyes or your body. It is something deeper, without a specific physical address. You look at your schedule and see a long day ahead — and the first thing you feel isn't readiness. It is: *Why must I start over again?*

You have tried. You know you have tried. But trying doesn't make it less tiring — sometimes it makes it heavier.

If you are there, this chapter is for you.

---

## The Name You Don't Have Yet

Certain states exist inside you very vividly — yet because they lack a clear name, you don't know how to speak about them. And because you cannot speak, you begin to doubt yourself: *Am I overreacting? Am I just being lazy?*

No. You are not lazy. And you are not overreacting.

Giving a name to an emotional state is the first step toward understanding and transforming it.
