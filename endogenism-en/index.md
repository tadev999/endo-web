# Endogenism - Returning to Yourself

Welcome to **Endogenism**.

This volume gathers reflections, essays, and notes on self-awareness, personal growth, and inner peace in the modern world.

## Book Navigation

1. **Stream 1: Naming** – Defining inner phenomena and emotional states.
2. **Stream 2: Building Foundations** – Core principles of self-observation and mental clarity.
3. **Stream 3: Small Actions** – Practicing presence and gradual progress in daily routines.
4. **Stream 4: Recognizing Value** – Uncovering intrinsic worth independent of external validation.
5. **Stream 5: Secular Sanctuary** – Cultivating a serene inner sanctuary amid life's complexity.
